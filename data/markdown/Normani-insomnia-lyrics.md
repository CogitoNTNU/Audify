# About

This song bio is unreviewed

Song Bio1 contributor

“Insomnia” is the sixth track off [Normani](https://genius.com/artists/Normani)’s debut album *[DOPAMINE](https://genius.com/albums/Normani/Dopamine)*. Inspired by the [Brandy](https://genius.com/artists/Brandy) song “[A Capella (Something’s Missing)](https://genius.com/Brandy-a-capella-somethings-missing-lyrics)”, the song features backing vocals from Brandy herself, as well as [Victoria Monét](https://genius.com/artists/Victoria-monet) (who both also co-wrote the song).

The song sees Normani singing about an ex-lover and their attempt to reconnect with her as she’s moving on. Normani realises how it has since made her reminisce more about their past relationship, now struggling to get over it.

Ask a question

What did Normani say about "Insomnia"?

Genius Answer

1 contributor

> “Insomnia” is a very beautiful song. At one point, it was actually taken off and put back on, and I’m glad it was. It feels nostalgic of what I grew up listening to. One of my favorite artists is also on the record, and she actually inspired it. As relevant as it felt then, it feels just as relevant now. And anytime you get that with a record, that means you have something special. It’s Brandy. She’s such a light and so encouraging. It was a full-circle moment, because it was inspired by her record “[A Capella] (Something Is Missing).” I wanted a lot of beautiful vocal production, layers, backgrounds, harmonies, countermelodies, and I think we did a good job of capturing that.

— via [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/entertainment/normani-new-album-dopamine)

+7

What did Normani say about working with Brandy?

Genius Answer

1 contributor

> I actually remember going into the studio with Stargate [a producer on the album] and being like, “I want to do something like this”—like Brandy’s “Something’s Missing.” That was everything I wanted to capture, so she was definitely the inspiration from the very beginning with that song. And then I decided to just shoot my shot, and ask if Brandy could help with the vocal production, because obviously she’s a master of her voice and of vocal production. She’s someone that I’ve always been influenced and inspired by since I was young—she was one of the very first artists that I fell in love with. So it felt like a full-circle moment. The fact that she actually loved the song just as much as I did and believed in it enough to want to jump on it really meant a lot to me. Sometimes you have to take a leap of faith.

— via [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/normani-dopamine-interview)
